Azion provides professional services delivered by its own specialists to help you migrate, configure, and operate your applications on the platform. Each service covers a different stage of that work:

- [Best Practices Review](/en/documentation/services/best-practices-review/): an evaluation of your infrastructure with recommendations on performance, security, and efficiency.
- [Business Events Support](/en/documentation/services/business-events-support/): assistance during events with expected traffic increase.
- [Instructor-Led Training](/en/documentation/services/instructor-led-training/): learning experiences adapted to your team's needs.
- [Integration Services](/en/documentation/services/integration-services/): migration of your content, web applications, and APIs to Azion.
- [Managed Configurations](/en/documentation/services/managed-configurations/): a team of engineers handling day-to-day configuration tasks for you.
- [Security Response Team](/en/documentation/services/security-response-team/): a managed service that applies technical measures on the platform to control security incidents.
- [Slack Channel](/en/documentation/services/slack-channel/): support through a written communication channel on Slack.
- [Technical Support](/en/documentation/services/support/): engineers specialized in performance, security, and high availability.
- [Technical Account Manager](/en/documentation/services/technical-account-manager/): a consultant providing strategic technical guidance.

To open a ticket or check your plan's support scope, see [Get help](/en/documentation/services/support/get-help/).
