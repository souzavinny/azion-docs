Your application is live on an Azion domain after the [first deploy](/en/documentation/get-started/first-deploy/). This guide puts it on your own domain: [add a custom domain](#add-a-custom-domain), then connect your DNS by [migrating your NS to Azion](#migrate-your-ns-to-azion) or by [pointing your domain to Azion](#point-your-domain-to-azion).

---

## Add a custom domain

Azion generates a domain for every application, in this format: `xxxxxxxxxx.map.azionedge.net`. It identifies your application on Azion and can't be modified, but you can associate your own domain and subdomains to it to handle user access.

### Access the Domains configuration page

[Access Azion Console](https://console.azion.com) and click the **Manage your Domains** card on the homepage. As an alternative, open the **Products menu** (the three horizontal lines in the upper-left corner) and select **Workloads**.

### Set up a new custom domain

Click **Add Domain** and fill in the fields:

- **Add Configuration Name**: type a name for your domain. You can use the same name as your domain name.
- [Digital Certificate](/en/documentation/secure/certificate-manager/): select the standard Azion certificate (SAN), a [Let's Encrypt certificate](/en/documentation/build/applications/guides/how-to-generate-a-lets-encrypt-certificate/), or any [third-party digital certificate](/en/documentation/secure/workloads/guides/create-a-digital-certificate/).
- [Canonical Names (CNAMES)](/en/documentation/build/applications/reference/domains/#cname): write all the CNAMES your domain has, one per line, no punctuation.
- **CNAME Access Only** switch: activate it if you want your users to access your application only by CNAME.
- **Application**: select the application your domain will be connected to.
- **Active** switch: it must be on.

Click **Save**. Your custom domain is now connected to the application.

:::note
Your root domain (example: `myapp.com`) and each of your subdomains (example: `user.myapp.com`) must be configured as a standalone domain. For other ways to configure domains and subdomains, visit the [Domains documentation](/en/documentation/secure/workloads/guides/configure-a-domain/).
:::

If you selected a Let's Encrypt certificate, you need to complete a specific process to configure the DNS settings. Check [How to generate a Let's Encrypt certificate](/en/documentation/build/applications/guides/how-to-generate-a-lets-encrypt-certificate/) for the details.

## Migrate your NS to Azion

With this option, Azion hosts and manages your DNS zones and records: you redirect the DNS resolution of your domain provider to Azion's authoritative nameservers (NS) through [Edge DNS](/en/documentation/secure/edge-dns/).

**Before you start:** you need a domain registered with a hosting service, an [application](/en/documentation/get-started/journeys/launch/), and a [custom domain](#add-a-custom-domain).

## Test it

Once your DNS changes propagate, your own domain answers with your application's content:

```bash
curl -I https://<your-domain>
# HTTP/2 200
```

Confirm the incoming traffic in [Real-Time Metrics](/en/documentation/observe/real-time-metrics/).
