## Purpose

A tutorial teaches the product by having the reader build something that works. The author chooses the goal, guarantees the result, and keeps every decision away from the reader.

## When to use

- The reader is new and does not yet know what they want. You pick a goal for them, and they learn the product on the way to it.
- Do not use a tutorial for a reader who arrived with a task: that page is a [how-to guide](/en/documentation/style-guide/content/how-to-guides/).
- When in doubt, [Choose a content type](/en/documentation/style-guide/content/choose-a-content-type/) has the router.

## Register

Procedural: 20 words per sentence, one instruction per step, imperative and active. [Sentence structure](/en/documentation/style-guide/writing/sentence-structure/) holds the rules.

## Structure

**Required components**

- **Opening paragraph**: what the reader will build and roughly how long it takes.
- **Before you start**: the minimum real prerequisites, each a link or a one-line command.
- **Numbered steps**: one instruction each, each ending with a visible result.
- **What you built**: one paragraph that restates the result.
- **Next steps**: two or three links, each with a reason to click it.

**Optional components**

- **One concept sentence** with a link to a [concept](/en/documentation/style-guide/content/concept/) page, when a concept is genuinely required.
- **An image** as the visible result of a step, when words alone cannot show it.

## Template

Copy the template and replace each `<placeholder>`.

```md
[One paragraph: what the reader will build,
and roughly how long it takes.]

## Before you start

- <A prerequisite the page cannot supply, as a link or a one-line command>

## Step 1: <verb phrase>

<What to do. Then what the reader should see.>

## Step 2: <verb phrase>

<...>

## What you built

<Restate the result. One paragraph.>

## Next steps

- <Link>: <the reason to click it>
- <Link>: <the reason to click it>
```

## Rules

- **Keep the promise.** Every step works and every command was run before the page shipped.
- **Choose everything.** The goal, the interface, and every value. The reader is here to learn, not to choose.
- **Do not branch.** One path. Alternatives belong in a how-to guide, and tabs are a branch.
- **Do not explain.** One sentence and a link when a concept is genuinely required.
- **Show the result of every step.** Command output, a screenshot, a URL, or a value that changed.
- **Keep tutorials scarce.** One per product area. Each one breaks on some future release.
