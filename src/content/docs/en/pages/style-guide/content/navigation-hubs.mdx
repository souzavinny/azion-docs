## Purpose

A navigation hub sends the reader deeper into the documentation. It carries links and one sentence of orientation, and nothing else.

It applies **no** Diátaxis base form. A hub does not teach, instruct, describe, or explain: it routes. That is why it has no sentence budget for prose it should not be carrying.

## When to use

- Write a hub when a section holds more pages than a sidebar makes legible.
- Write one for a section that gathers pages from several products, such as a solutions or architectures index.

Do not write a hub when:

- The section is a product. A product opens with an [Overview](/en/documentation/style-guide/content/overview/), which orients and links.
- The page would explain what the section is about at length. That is a [concept page](/en/documentation/style-guide/content/concept/).
- The sidebar already makes the pages findable. A hub that duplicates the sidebar is a second thing to maintain and a second thing to go stale.

## Register

Descriptive: 25 words per sentence, active voice, passive only when the actor is unknown. [Sentence structure](/en/documentation/style-guide/writing/sentence-structure/) holds the rules.

## Structure

**Required components**

- **One sentence of orientation**: what this section covers and who it is for.
- **The links**: grouped, each with a one-line description saying what the reader gets.

**Optional components**

- **Groups**: when the set of links has more than one theme, each group gets a heading.

A card-grid hub is the one place a documentation page sets `type: homepage` in its frontmatter. Every other page omits the field. See [Frontmatter](/en/documentation/style-guide/conventions/frontmatter/).

## Template

```md
<One sentence: what this section covers and who it is for.>

## <Group name>

- [<Page title>](<url>): <what the reader gets from it, in one line>.
- [<Page title>](<url>): <what the reader gets from it, in one line>.

## <Next group name>

- [<Page title>](<url>): <what the reader gets from it, in one line>.
```

## Rules

- **Make every link earn its place.** A hub that lists everything ranks nothing.
- **Write descriptions that differentiate.** Say what this page gives that its neighbours do not.
- **Do not explain here.** One sentence of orientation; the rest belongs in a [concept page](/en/documentation/style-guide/content/concept/).
- **Group when the list passes seven**, by what the reader is trying to do.
- **Keep it in step with the section.** Update the hub in the same change that adds a page.
