## Purpose

A how-to guide takes a reader who has a problem to a solved problem. The reader already knows what they want. The page removes the obstacles between them and the result; it does not teach.

## When to use

- The reader arrived with a specific task and a real situation.
- Do not use a how-to for a reader who is learning the product: that page is a [tutorial](/en/documentation/style-guide/content/tutorials/).
- A how-to whose task is a fix is a [troubleshooting page](/en/documentation/style-guide/content/troubleshooting/).
- When in doubt, [Choose a content type](/en/documentation/style-guide/content/choose-a-content-type/) has the router.

## Register

Procedural: 20 words per sentence, one instruction per step, imperative and active. [Sentence structure](/en/documentation/style-guide/writing/sentence-structure/) holds the rules.

## Structure

**Required components**

- **Outcome paragraph**: the first sentence states what the reader has when they finish.
- **Prerequisites**: one tip aside near the top, never a section.
- **Task sections**: imperative verb-phrase headings with numbered steps, one instruction each.
- **End state**: the last step or the closing states what the reader sees when the task worked.
- **Closing tip**: one or two links to where the reader goes next.

**Optional components**

- **Tabs by interface**, when the task runs through more than one interface.
- **One aside** holding the alternative path, when the task genuinely branches.

## Template

Copy the template and replace each `<placeholder>`.

```md
[One paragraph: the outcome. Start with the result,
not the background.]

:::tip
Prerequisites, if any. One aside, not a section.
:::

## <Imperative verb phrase that names the task>

Steps. Tabbed by interface if the task has more
than one. The last step states what the reader sees.

## <Next task, if the page covers a sequence>

---

:::tip
Where to go next. One or two links.
:::
```

Separate the major sections of the page with a `---` rule, and never place one directly after the frontmatter.

## Rules

- **Name the task in the title and the headings.** An imperative verb phrase, never a gerund.
- **One imperative instruction per step.** A step with two actions is a step where the second is skipped.
- **State the end state first.** The opening sentence says what the reader will have.
- **Branch where the task branches.** Tabs for Console, CLI, and API, never three sequential sections.
- **Use one verb per action**, on every mention across the page.
- **Send a goal that crosses products** to a [multi-product guide](/en/documentation/style-guide/content/multi-product-guides/).
- **Link out for third-party products.** Name the vendor's step; do not document their interface.
- **Do not teach.** One sentence of context, then the steps.
