## Purpose

An Overview page is the root of a product section. It states what the product is, what it does, and when a reader would use it. It applies the [reference](/en/documentation/style-guide/content/reference/) base form.

Every product has one. It is the page a reader lands on from search, from the sidebar, and from another product that links to this one.

## When to use

- Write one Overview for every product, as the first page of its section.
- Write it before any other page in that section. The Overview decides what the section is about.

Do not write an Overview when:

- The subject is a module or a feature. That belongs in a [reference page](/en/documentation/style-guide/content/reference/) inside the product section.
- The page would walk the reader through a task. That is a [how-to guide](/en/documentation/style-guide/content/how-to-guides/), linked from here.
- The page would only list links. That is a [navigation hub](/en/documentation/style-guide/content/navigation-hubs/).

## Register

Descriptive: 25 words per sentence, active voice, passive only when the actor is unknown. [Sentence structure](/en/documentation/style-guide/writing/sentence-structure/) holds the rules.

## Structure

**Required components**

- **Definition block**: `**<Product>** is a <capability definition of roughly 10 to 15 words>.` Then the problems it solves, as a short benefit list in behavior terms.
- **CTAs**: a primary `LinkButton` labeled **Get started** and a secondary one labeled `<Product> reference`.
- **Capability sections**: one `##` per major capability or module. Noun-phrase headings; each section holds a short paragraph or a table.
- **Interfaces**: the list `You can create and manage <Product> using:`, with Azion Console, API, Azion CLI, and Terraform as links.
- **Closing**: `## Related resources`, with links shaped `[Title](/path/) - reason`, covering get started and the key concepts.

The sections keep this order.

## Template

Copy the template and replace each `<placeholder>`:

```mdx
import LinkButton from 'azion-webkit/linkbutton'

**<Product>** is a <capability definition of roughly 10 to 15 words>.

Use <Product> to:

- <A problem it solves, in behavior terms>
- <...>

<LinkButton label="Get started" link="<get started URL>" />
<LinkButton severity="secondary" label="<Product> reference" link="<reference URL>" />

---

## <Capability or module, as a noun phrase>

<A short paragraph or a table.>

## <Capability or module, as a noun phrase>

<...>

---

## Interfaces

You can create and manage <Product> using:

- [Azion Console](<url>)
- [API](<url>)
- [Azion CLI](<url>)
- [Terraform](<url>)

---

## Related resources

- [Get started with <Product>](<url>) - <why the reader would follow it>
- [<Key concept>](<url>) - <why the reader would follow it>
```

## Rules

- **Never instruct.** No numbered steps, no procedures, no code walkthroughs. An overview that instructs is a [get started page](/en/documentation/style-guide/content/get-started/) filed wrong.
- **No quality adjectives.** Say what the product does and where it stops.
- **The title is the product name, a noun.** Not "documentation", not a gerund.
- **Name the product in the first sentence.** Not the problem space.
- **Send the reader to [Get started](/en/documentation/style-guide/content/get-started/)**, near the top and again at the end.
- **Mark gaps, never fill them.** A mandated slot the input does not supply — a CTA, the interfaces list — gets a `[GAP: ...]` marker, never silent omission, never invention.
- **Keep it scannable.** Tables for anything enumerable, one or two sentences between them.

## Examples

This excerpt of an Overview page for **Data Stream** shows the definition block with its CTAs, the interfaces list, and the closing. The slots the input did not supply carry `[GAP: ...]` markers:

```mdx
**Data Stream** is an Observe product that feeds stream processing, SIEM, and big data platforms with your event logs in real time.

Use Data Stream to:

- Collect, group, and transmit raw logs to a destination that you choose.
- Choose the Azion products and domains to collect logs from.
- Choose the variables that the stream uses.

<LinkButton label="Get started" link="/en/documentation/products/observe/data-stream/first-steps/" />

[GAP: secondary CTA — permalink for a Data Stream reference page]

---

## Interfaces

You can create and manage Data Stream using:

- [GAP: management interfaces and their links — Console, API, CLI, Terraform availability not given]

---

## Related resources

- [Data Stream first steps](/en/documentation/products/observe/data-stream/first-steps/) - Begin to use Data Stream.
- [Configure Data Stream main settings](/en/documentation/products/guides/use-data-stream/) - Configure the main settings of a stream.
```
